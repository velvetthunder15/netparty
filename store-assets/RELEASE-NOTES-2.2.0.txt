NetParty 2.2.0

- Now on HBO Max, Crunchyroll and SonyLIV (start a party from the NetParty icon on an episode or movie).
- Waits for everyone: if a friend's video is still loading, the party pauses and plays on together once it's ready.
- If someone drops out while you're watching, the party pauses until they're back (or press Resume).
- See who's typing, @mention friends, and hide spoilers with ||double bars|| or the Spoiler switch.
- Edit or delete your messages, and pin one message for the whole party.
- In voice chat the video gets quieter while friends talk, then goes back to your volume.
- New Settings in the chat panel and the popup to switch these off.
- 48 new HD profile pictures, or upload your own photo (only people in your party see it).
- Voice and video stay connected through the relay in long parties and after a reconnect.
- Everything stays end-to-end encrypted, like the rest of the chat.
