NetParty 1.9.1 - release notes ("What's new")

=== Edge Add-ons / Opera (under 500 characters) ===
- Resize the popped-out camera window from any edge or corner.
- Quick messages stack: lines you send within a minute show your name and picture only once.

=== Firefox Add-ons (AMO) ===
What's new in 1.9.1

- The popped-out camera window can now be resized from any edge or corner, not just the small corner grip. Tiles stay 16:9 and the size is remembered for the tab.
- Messages sent close together stack: when someone sends several lines within a minute, their name and picture show once at the top instead of on every line.

Nothing changes about what NetParty sends or stores.
