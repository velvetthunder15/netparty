NetParty 2.0.1 (changes since 1.9.1)

New
• Sync any video: on other video sites, click the NetParty icon and press Sync this tab to start a party. NetParty only runs on that tab after you click.
• Friends open your invite, go to the video, and press Join party on this tab.
• Optional Always allow on this site, so reloads keep the party.
• Videos inside another site's player frame work after you allow that player's site.
• Theater mode: the video fills the window next to the chat.
• Smoother sync with matched playback speed. Live streams sync play and pause.
• A warning with Open host's page when a friend is on a different video.

Improved
• The chat panel and popup now use the Inter font on every system.
• The page address of an any-video party is end-to-end encrypted, like chat.

Fixed
• Sync this tab now works when the video is paused.
