NetParty 2.0.0 - release notes ("What's new")

=== Chrome Web Store / Edge Add-ons / Opera (under 500 characters) ===
- New: sync videos on sites you choose. Open a video, click NetParty, press Sync this tab. Friends open the invite, press Open the video, then Join party on this tab.
- NetParty runs on such a site only after you click (or with Always allow on this site).
- The page address is end-to-end encrypted.
- Theater mode keeps the chat beside the video.

=== Firefox Add-ons (AMO) ===
What's new in 2.0.0

- Sync videos on sites you choose: on a site that isn't Netflix, JioHotstar, YouTube or Prime Video, click the NetParty icon and press Sync this tab. NetParty finds the video and starts a party.
- Friends open the invite link, press Open the video, then click the NetParty icon on that page and press Join party on this tab.
- NetParty only runs on such a site after you click, or on sites you set to Always allow (optional permission for that one site, removable at any time).
- Players inside another site's frame ask for access to that player's site first.
- If a friend is on a different video, NetParty tells them and offers to open the host's page.
- Theater mode: the video fills the window next to the chat.
- Live streams sync play and pause.

Data: for these parties, the page address and the video's length are shared with your party, end-to-end encrypted. Our server can't read them. See https://netparty.tech/privacy
