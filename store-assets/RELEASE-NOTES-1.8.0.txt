=== Firefox Add-ons (AMO) ===
What's new in 1.8.0

- Prime Video: parties now work on primevideo.com and on Amazon's Prime Video pages (amazon.com, .co.uk, .de, .in and other stores). Play, pause and seek stay in sync, and there's a Start a NetParty button next to Play. This is new and hasn't been tested on a live Prime account yet, so please report anything that doesn't sync.
- Video chat: turn your camera on next to voice chat. Small tiles show everyone with a camera on; drag them anywhere or fold them away. Up to 4 cameras at 640x360. Video goes peer to peer between friends (its setup is end-to-end encrypted like chat), so on strict networks (some college or office Wi-Fi) it may not connect; there's no relay server yet.
- Light mode: the chat panel and popup follow your system's light or dark setting. Choose Light or Dark under Settings to override it.
- Your own messages now show your picture and name, like everyone else's.
- Sharper icons on high-resolution screens.
- Security: invite links and "follow the host" only ever lead to Netflix, JioHotstar, YouTube or Prime Video titles.

The camera is only used after you press the camera button, and it turns off when you press it again or leave voice.
