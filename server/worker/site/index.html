FAQ

Questions, answered.

Is NetParty free?

Yes. NetParty is free with no account or sign-up. Everyone just needs their own subscription to the service you're watching.

Does it stream the video to my friends?

No. Each person plays the title from their own streaming account. NetParty only syncs play, pause, seek and the episode, plus your chat.

Which sites and browsers work?

Netflix, JioHotstar, YouTube and Prime Video on a computer, in Google Chrome, Brave and other Chromium browsers, or in Microsoft Edge from the Edge Add-ons store. Firefox and Opera are coming soon. Phones and TVs aren't supported.

How do voice and video chat work?

Click Join voice chat in the panel and allow your microphone, then press the camera button if you want video. Audio and video travel directly between the people in the party, and the connection setup is encrypted too.

What happens to our chat?

Chat is end-to-end encrypted. Your browser encrypts every message, GIF, reaction, name and profile picture with a key that lives only in the invite link, so our server only ever relays and stores scrambled data. Parties and their encrypted messages are deleted when the party ends, or within 6 hours.

Why do friends need the full link?

The invite link carries your party's encryption key after the # sign, which is never sent to any server. A bare party code can't unlock the chat, so always share the full link.

Do I need to update?

Parties now need NetParty 1.7 or newer. If a friend's version is older, they'll be asked to update, and installing the latest download takes 30 seconds.

Can someone else control playback?

By default anyone can play, pause or seek. The host can switch on 'Only I can play, pause and skip' in the popup.
