NetParty

Privacy Policy

Last updated 27 October 2026

NetParty is a free browser extension that lets friends watch a video together in sync, with text and voice chat. It is run by Chirag Rasane. This policy explains what the extension and its sync server handle, and why.

No accounts, no tracking

NetParty has no sign-up and no accounts. It has no analytics, advertising or tracking code, and we never sell or share your data.

What the extension handles

  • Display name. The name you choose, shown to others in your party. It is stored in your browser and sent to the sync server while you're in a party.
  • Chat messages. What you type in the party chat, relayed to everyone in that party.
  • Party and playback state. The party code, the title and ID of what's playing, and play, pause and seek events with timestamps, so everyone stays in sync.
  • Voice chat. Voice runs peer to peer between party members over WebRTC. The server only passes the connection setup messages that let browsers find each other. Your audio is not sent to or recorded by our server. Microphone access is requested only when you turn voice on.
  • Local settings. Your name, server setting and party state are kept in your browser's extension storage and session storage.

NetParty does not read your streaming account details, passwords, payment information or viewing history. It works only on the supported streaming sites listed on the home page.

How long we keep it

A party, with its member names and up to its last 150 chat messages, is kept on the sync server while it's active, so people who reload can rejoin. It is deleted when the host ends the party, and any inactive party is removed after at most 6 hours. Basic request logs kept by our hosting provider follow its standard retention.

Where it's processed

The sync server runs on Cloudflare Workers. Cloudflare processes connection data, such as IP addresses, to deliver the service under its own privacy policy.

Children

NetParty is not directed at children under 13, and we don't knowingly collect information from them.
